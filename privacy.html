We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves. There are no accounts. Your progress, settings and purchase status are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows rewarded ads before boss fights and when you choose to watch an ad to revive. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • device and app information such as model, OS version, language and approximate location (derived from IP address);
  • ad impression and click records.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies and is not sent to us. See "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices:

  • iPhone / iPad: on first launch the system asks whether to allow tracking. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalised. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch a consent form lets you consent, decline or manage options. You can change your choice later in the game settings.
  • Certain US states: the consent form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your data.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in system settings.
  • No ads at all: the one-time in-game unlock (NT$90 or local equivalent) removes all ads, and no ads are loaded afterwards.

In-app purchases

The unlock and paid revives are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their privacy policies. We never receive your card or account details, only a confirmation that this device has made the purchase.

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
